Microsoft Fabric

What Microsoft Fabric means for finance teams.

Fabric brings data engineering, warehousing, real-time analytics and business intelligence into a unified environment—but value still depends on good architecture and governance.

By Mahdi Aghaei8 October 20266 min read

One platform, connected workloads

Finance teams often depend on data from ERP, CRM, payroll, spreadsheets and operational systems. A unified platform can reduce duplicated movement and create reusable, governed datasets.

Where it can help

  • Consolidating reporting data from multiple entities
  • Automating repeatable data preparation
  • Creating governed finance and operational models
  • Supporting Power BI with a scalable foundation
  • Improving lineage and ownership

Start with a use case

A platform programme should begin with a valuable, bounded use case: management accounts, sales margin, cash flow or operational performance. Prove the model, governance and delivery approach before expanding.
