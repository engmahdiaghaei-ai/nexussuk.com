What this website uses

The site stores your analytics preference in your browser using local storage. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or declined optional analytics.

Optional analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics may be enabled for privacy-focused aggregate measurement. Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are designed to load only after you accept analytics. If their IDs are not configured, they do not load.

Your choice

You can accept or decline optional analytics from the banner. To change your decision, use the button below and choose again.

Browser controls

You can also clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking technologies may affect measurement but should not prevent access to the site's core content or enquiry form.

Changes

We will update this policy when analytics tools or their purposes materially change.
