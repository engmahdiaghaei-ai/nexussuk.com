Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This practical policy should be reviewed when the registered office, company number, processors or services change. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

Who we are

NEXUSSUK LTD, trading as Nexus, provides software development, data and business intelligence services. For privacy questions, contact Info@Nexussuk.com.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, enquiry content, service interests, communication history and limited technical analytics data where you consent.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange discovery conversations
  • To prepare proposals and provide requested services
  • To operate, secure and improve this website
  • To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations

Legal bases

Depending on the context, we rely on steps taken at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations or consent.

Service providers

Website enquiries are processed using Formspree. Website hosting is provided through GitHub Pages. If configured, analytics may be provided by Cloudflare, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. These providers may process technical information under their own terms.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected and applicable obligations. We use proportionate organisational and technical measures, but no internet service is completely risk-free.

Your rights

UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Rights can depend on the circumstances.
