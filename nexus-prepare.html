Before you get started

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Nexus an accountancy or payroll service?

No. Nexus sells software and provides software development and BI consultancy. Nexus Prepare is a document workflow product; it does not mean Nexus is offering bookkeeping, payroll or tax services.

Can I open the web application now?

Yes. Use “Open Nexus Prepare” or “Client login” to access the web application. An account and an authorised workspace are required. For a guided demonstration or activation, contact Nexus.

Where can I download the mobile app?

The capture application is currently a prototype for device testing. Ask us for the current testing route. We will add genuine App Store and Google Play links when those releases are available.

Does the software replace a reviewer?

No. Extraction helps reduce manual entry, but the original evidence and extracted fields should be checked before approval or publishing.

Are these the same features as Dext?

No. The price benchmark uses Dext’s published base business pricing and matching document/user capacities. Connectors, automation, extraction credits, add-ons and other capabilities differ. Check the current Nexus demonstration and your written order.

How do annual prices work?

Annual mode shows the rounded monthly equivalent and the exact amount billed for a year. Monthly document allowances remain monthly. VAT is charged only where applicable and is confirmed in your quote.
