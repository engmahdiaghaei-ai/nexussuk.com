Nexus Prepare / Integration approach

Keep your system.
Connect the paperwork.

Nexus Prepare is the document layer between mobile capture and your accounting workflow. Our integration focus is Sage, Xero and the systems your organisation already uses.

Capture → review → hand-off

Middleware, not another
accounting service.

Keep the original evidence and review the extracted fields before any onward transfer. We scope the receiving system, the mapping and the publishing behaviour with your team.

01 / SOURCE

Capture

Send invoices, receipts and related documents from mobile or web.

02 / PREPARE

Review

Check the document type, counterparty, amounts and supporting evidence.

03 / MAPPING

Validate

Agree the destination fields, tax-code mapping and duplicate checks.

04 / DESTINATION

Hand off

Use the connector or export route confirmed for your deployment.

Know the boundary

The right connection.
For the right edition.

Integration planning — not a live connector availability list
Destination What we scope Availability
Sage Exact product, regional edition, document types, supported fields and the evidence hand-off. Confirm the edition and demonstrate the supported route before ordering.
Xero Organisation access, invoice or bill mapping, attachment handling and error recovery. Integration target. Request a current compatibility demonstration.
Other platforms Available API or import format, permissions, validation rules and practical delivery scope. Assessed individually. No universal connector is promised.

Sage and Xero are third-party trademarks. Their mention does not imply partnership, certification or an active connection in your workspace. Connector availability, region and subscription requirements must be confirmed in writing.
