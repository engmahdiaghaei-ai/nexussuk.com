Accessibility

We want this website to work for everyone.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Our approach

We aim to provide clear structure, keyboard access, readable contrast, descriptive image text, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Some animated product demonstrations and third-party destinations may not provide the same level of accessibility. We are continuing to review content as the site develops.

Feedback and alternative formats

If you cannot access information or need it in another format, email Info@Nexussuk.com. Please include the page and the difficulty you encountered. We will make reasonable efforts to help.

Technical compatibility

The website is designed for current versions of major browsers and responsive devices. Very old browsers may not support every visual effect, but core content should remain available.
