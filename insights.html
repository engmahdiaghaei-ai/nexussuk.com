Practical reporting guide

How to measure whether a dashboard is worth building

Before building the report, agree which decision it supports and who makes it. For a weekly operations report, measure the time spent gathering and reconciling inputs over several representative reporting cycles. Keep time waiting for data separate from time spent processing it.

Illustrative example: If compiling a weekly pack takes 90 minutes before a redesign and 35 minutes during an equivalent pilot, the observed difference is 55 minutes, or approximately 61% less preparation time per cycle. Those figures are an example, not a client outcome or a forecast.

Check the figures reconcile, record the cost of data preparation and maintenance, and ask whether the dashboard changes a decision. Performance Analyzer can help diagnose report interactions, but it does not measure time saved by the business workflow.
