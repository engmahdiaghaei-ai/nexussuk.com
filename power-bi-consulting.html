Project questions

Before you commission a Power BI dashboard

Use these questions to prepare a brief for your finance, operations or reporting team.

What determines the cost?

The main factors are the number and condition of data sources, the metrics to reconcile, refresh and access requirements, and who needs training. Describe one reporting workflow first so the initial scope and any later work can be quoted separately.

Can you use our Excel or SQL Server data?

Excel files and SQL Server can both be starting points. Share a source list and a redacted sample, not credentials or live customer data. For a source on your internal network, include refresh and access arrangements in the scope.

How do we agree the dashboard is ready?

Pick representative reporting periods, reconcile headline figures against an agreed source, and test the refresh and access with the people who will use it. For example, a monthly sales dashboard could be checked against the sales ledger for the same closed period.
