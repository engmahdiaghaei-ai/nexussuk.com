Payroll

A practical UK payroll compliance checklist.

Consistent inputs, documented checks and timely submissions help reduce payroll errors and avoid unnecessary pressure.

By Niloofar Zare8 October 20265 min read
General information only: payroll obligations depend on your circumstances. Check current GOV.UK guidance or obtain professional advice.

Before processing

  • Confirm starters, leavers and employee detail changes
  • Collect approved hours, overtime, bonuses and deductions
  • Review tax codes and HMRC notices
  • Confirm pension and statutory payment information

Before submission

  • Reconcile gross-to-net totals
  • Review unusual changes from the prior period
  • Check PAYE, National Insurance and pension values
  • Submit the Full Payment Submission on or before payday in normal circumstances

After payroll

Issue payslips, arrange payments, retain appropriate records and track upcoming PAYE and pension deadlines. Investigate corrections promptly and document the resolution.
