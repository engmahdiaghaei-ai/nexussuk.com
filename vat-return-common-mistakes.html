VAT

Common VAT return mistakes—and how to reduce them.

Good records and repeatable review controls are the foundation of a more accurate VAT process.

By Niloofar Zare8 October 20265 min read
General information only: VAT treatment can be complex and fact-specific. Use current HMRC guidance and professional advice for your circumstances.

Weak source records

Missing invoices, duplicated transactions and incomplete descriptions make VAT treatment harder to support. Keep digital records organised and reconcile control accounts.

Incorrect tax treatment

Zero-rated, exempt, outside-scope and standard-rated transactions are not interchangeable. Ensure tax codes reflect the actual supply and supporting evidence.

Claiming input tax without support

Review whether a valid VAT invoice is held and whether any restrictions apply before reclaiming VAT.

Skipping the final review

  • Compare totals with prior periods
  • Review large or unusual transactions
  • Reconcile VAT control accounts
  • Confirm the correct reporting period
  • Retain evidence for adjustments
